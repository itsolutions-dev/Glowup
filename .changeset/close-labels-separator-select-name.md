`BottomSheet` and `Drawer` `closeText` (the close button and the scrim) and `Drawer` `backText`; `Breadcrumbs` `separatorText`, text between the items instead of the glyph. A `Select` is named by its label and then its value ("Contact type, Email"), so it is found by its label. A `Dialog` whose backdrop does not close it draws an inert scrim that never takes focus.
