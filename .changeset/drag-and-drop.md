Drag and drop, with no new dependency: `DragDropProvider`, `Draggable`, `Droppable` and `useDragDrop`, on iOS, Android and the web, by touch and by mouse. A touch lifts an item after resting on it for 250 ms (a swipe still scrolls), a mouse after moving 4 px (a click still clicks); a pressable child such as a `Card` keeps its tap. The item stays in place, dimmed, while a preview follows the pointer through the `Portal`, and a line marks where it would land. `onDragEnd({ active, over })` reports the drop, with `over.index` counted without the dragged item, so reordering a list and moving between lists read the same way. Zones nest (the innermost one that `accept`s the item wins, and a zone never takes the draggable it sits in), `orientation` sets the axis, Escape cancels on the web, and a touch lift reports a `longPress` to `onInteractionFeedback`. `useDragScroll()`, spread on the `ScrollView` or `ScrollArea` holding the zones, locks it while an item is held, scrolls it by itself near its top and bottom edges and keeps the zones measured as it scrolls; zones that move during a drag are measured again. For zones that place by position (a grid), `over.rect` gives the dragged item's preview relative to the zone and `over.zone` the zone's size, `indicator={false}` turns the insertion line off, and `useDragState(select)` reads the drag in progress, re-rendering only when the selected value changes.
