`GridLayout` arranges its items with `editable`: drag an item to move it, select it and pull its handles (four edges, four corners) to resize it, drop a `Draggable` from elsewhere (a palette) to add one. Nothing is compacted or pushed: a move or a resize onto another item is refused, and a palette drop lands on the first free row at or below where it was let go. `onLayoutChange(layout)` reports a move or a resize, `onDrop(item, placement)` a palette drop (`dropSize(item)` says how big it lands, or `null` to refuse it), `selected` / `onSelect` hold the selection (uncontrolled when left out), and `spareRows` (default 4) keeps empty rows below the lowest item. While dragging, the empty cells and the landing place are drawn, in the error colour where it is refused. The items take no taps of their own and the grid never stacks while editable. It uses the app's `DragDropProvider` when there is one, so a palette can share it, and brings its own otherwise. New exports for code that edits layouts: `gridItemsOverlap`, `gridItemFits`, `gridFreeRow`, `gridCellAt`, `gridPitch`, `resizeGridItem` and the `GridHandle` / `GridPlacement` types. `Droppable` takes `onDrop`, called before the provider's `onDragEnd` (now optional), and `useHasDragDrop()` says whether a provider is above.
