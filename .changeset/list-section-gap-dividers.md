`ListSection` takes `gap` (`"xs"` to `"xl"`, or a number: the space between items) and `showDividers` (a rule between consecutive items, `n - 1` for `n`). `divider` is unchanged: the one rule after the last item.
With `ListItem variant="standard"` they make a plain list, like a sidebar's.
